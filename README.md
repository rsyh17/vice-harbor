Vice Harbor is an original top-down open-world crime game that plays offline in a desktop browser from its own files — no libraries, no network. A full city of named buildings, parks and a beach: explore on foot or steal any car, with live traffic, wandering pedestrians, fists and a pistol, a five-star wanted system with police chases that can end busted or wasted, and a five-job story for fixer Dewi that ends with a harbor escape. HUD with health, money, wanted stars and a live minimap; progress saves to localStorage.

## Play it

```sh
node serve.ts        # then open http://localhost:8080
```

Move with WASD, E gets in and out of cars, J punches, K shoots, Space is the handbrake, P pauses and saves.
